Our Organization

Built in Malawi. Built for Malawi.

Rugare Mental Health Organisation is a youth-led, registered nonprofit founded in 2023, dedicated to tackling Malawi's pressing mental health challenges. We work to increase awareness, break stigma, and foster positive attitudes toward mental well-being — in communities, schools, and workplaces.

Today, we bring that same passion and expertise to corporate Malawi — helping organizations build psychologically healthy workplaces where people, and business, can truly thrive.

Our Mission

To empower individuals and organizations across Malawi with the knowledge, resources, and support to prioritize mental health and promote holistic well-being.

Our Vision

A mentally strong Malawi where people understand the crucial role mental health plays in overall well-being — leading to healthier, happier, and more productive lives for all.

Our Approach

Evidence-based, culturally grounded, and practically focused. Our trainers bring real clinical insight, deep empathy, and a genuine understanding of the Malawian context.

Our Values

Empathy · Empowerment · Inclusivity · Integrity · Collaboration · Community Engagement — not just words, but the foundation of every session we deliver.

Category 1 — Corporate & Workplace Wellness

Strategic programs for HR teams, leaders, and the whole organization

Employee Wellness & Corporate Mental Health

A flagship program helping organizations build a culture where mental health is openly supported. Covers workplace triggers, burnout, work-life balance, financial wellness, anxiety and depression awareness, leadership styles, and practical tools staff can use immediately.

2 Days

Implementing Wellbeing & Mental Health in the Workplace

A practical guide for HR professionals and wellness leaders on designing, launching, and sustaining effective mental health initiatives. Covers the full business case, stress management strategy, program design, and common pitfalls to avoid.

2 Days

Workplace Recovery Action Plan (WRAP)

A structured, people-centered program grounded in 5 Key Recovery Concepts: Hope, Personal Responsibility, Education, Self-Advocacy, and Support. Equips teams with personal wellness toolboxes, daily maintenance plans, and clear crisis strategies.

4 Days

Mental Health at Work for Managers

Empowers managers to spot early warning signs, hold sensitive conversations, and support team members compassionately. Research shows poor employee mental health leads to an 80% decrease in concentration and a 57% drop in multi-tasking ability.

2 Days

The Manager's Role in Resilience

High-impact training helping managers model resilience, build psychologically safe teams, and create environments where staff thrive under pressure. Designed for HR professionals, team leads, and OD specialists.

1 Day

Becoming a Workplace Wellbeing Champion

Trains selected staff to become internal mental health advocates and peer supporters. Covers facilitation basics, resource navigation, and how to drive grassroots wellbeing culture — a cost-effective way to embed wellbeing organization-wide.

2 Days

Category 2 — Personal Development & Targeted Health

Programs for individuals, teams, and specific employee groups

Addressing Women's Health

Explores the unique mental health challenges faced by women — hormonal changes, work-life balance, caregiving stress, menopause, pregnancy, and trauma. Combines clinical insight with practical self-care and organizational support strategies.

1 Day

Addressing Men's Health

A safe, stigma-free space to explore the real pressures men face — emotional expression, work stress, financial strain, and societal expectations. Helps male employees build healthy coping mechanisms and connect with support before challenges escalate.

1 Day

Strengthening Personal Resilience

Teaches proven techniques to bounce back from setbacks, manage pressure, and maintain wellbeing through difficult periods. Participants leave with personalized resilience plans and actionable coping strategies for both work and life.

1 Day

Energy, Focus & Getting Organised

A practical workshop on managing attention in today's high-distraction workplace. Covers energy management, procrastination, email management, and optimizing daily routines for peak mental performance, grounded in modern productivity science.

1.5 Days

Strengths & Confidence

Helps participants identify core strengths, build genuine self-confidence, and develop a positive self-image. Uses the GROW model to set strength-based goals aligned with personal values — transforming how employees see themselves at work.

1 Day

Substance Abuse Awareness in the Workplace

Raises awareness of the signs, risks, and workplace impacts of substance abuse. Equips supervisors and staff to respond compassionately and constructively while supporting the development of prevention and employee support policies.

Half Day

Mindfulness at Work

An accessible introduction to mindfulness — proven to reduce stress and anxiety, improve emotional regulation, and boost mental clarity. Immediately applicable daily exercises suitable for all staff levels, including those with no prior experience.

1 Day

Category 3 — Core Mental Health Skills

Specialist training for deeper mental health capability

Mental Health Awareness

An introductory workshop for all staff levels designed to break stigma, increase understanding of common mental health conditions, and build confidence in supporting colleagues. The ideal foundation for any organization starting its mental health journey.

1 Day

Mental Health First Aid (MHFA)

An internationally recognized, skills-based course teaching participants how to recognize mental health crises and provide initial support. Covers depression, anxiety, psychosis, and substance use. Participants receive a certificate as qualified Mental Health First Aiders.

2 Days

Youth Mental Health First Aid

Designed for parents, teachers, coaches, and caregivers working with young people aged 12–18. Covers warning signs, supportive conversations, bullying, self-harm, eating disorders, and social media impact. Ideal for organizations with school partnerships or youth programs.

2 Days

Applied Suicide Intervention Skills Training (ASIST)

The gold-standard two-day suicide intervention training. Participants learn to recognize signs of suicidal thoughts, conduct safe risk assessments, and develop effective intervention plans. Major international studies confirm ASIST significantly reduces suicidality.

2 Days

Wellbeing Skills: Next Steps in Employment

A recovery-oriented program supporting individuals with mental health challenges to return to or enter the workplace. Over 4 weeks, participants build confidence, develop coping strategies, understand their rights, and create personalized employment action plans.

4 Weeks
How It Works

Simple to engage. Powerful in impact.

  1. 1

    Initial Consultation

    We meet with your HR or leadership team to understand your organization's needs, culture, and specific wellbeing goals.

  2. 2

    Tailored Program Design

    We select and customize the right combination of programs from our portfolio — or design something entirely bespoke for your context.

  3. 3

    Delivery at Your Premises

    Our facilitators come to you. All you need is a suitable room, a projector or screen, and flip chart materials.

  4. 4

    Post-training resources, referral guidance, and availability for consultancy, audits, and long-term partnership.
