What we do

Our Impact Areas

Through creativity, community, and conversation, we design safe, youth-led spaces that break social stigma and make emotional wellness accessible across Malawi.

Mental Health Clubs

Creating peer-led spaces in schools where young people access mental health literacy, learn healthy coping strategies, and build resilience through open conversations and support.

Community Engagement & Digital Awareness

Creating safe, stigma-free spaces through community events such as family wellness days, mental health forums, school outreach sessions, and interactive activities, alongside webinars, digital campaigns, and our Mind Matters Podcast to increase awareness and normalize conversations around mental health.

TulaNkhawa Talks (Psychosocial Support Services)

Providing accessible and confidential psychosocial support and referral pathways, giving young people a safe space to seek help, heal, and navigate challenges without fear of judgment or stigma.
