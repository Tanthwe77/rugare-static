Our Programs

Creative, community-centred initiatives designed to make mental health accessible, relatable, and stigma-free.

Programs in action

Mental Health Clubs in Schools

We establish and support mental health clubs in secondary schools across Malawi. These clubs provide a safe, supportive space where students can learn about mental health, share their experiences, and develop coping skills — all guided by trained peer facilitators.

Vision Board Sessions

Our vision board sessions blend creativity with conversation. Young people reflect on their goals, identity, and well-being — building hope, connection, and practical next steps for everyday life.

We run sessions in schools and communities, adapting the format to the needs of each group.
